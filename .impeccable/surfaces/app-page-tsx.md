Scope: the whole BatchMail app (sign-in, Recipients, Message, Review & Send, Help). Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.

Audience and task: CNCP officers working through a three-step send under time pressure. Must stay intact: email-sending logic, outgoing templates, tour element IDs, the light editor and preview surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every recipient is a star. A galaxy-purple working tool whose sky stays behind the task and earns its one moment when a batch goes out. It refuses a glassy dark dashboard with neon edges, and it refuses space costume on the controls.

OWN-WORLD: Deep aubergine sky, solid violet-tinted surfaces, an ultraviolet primary, starlight gold reserved for "done" and "delivered", a deep violet app header in both modes. Geist throughout, Geist Mono for code and logs. Standard buttons, inputs, tabs, and tables.

STORY: The officer sees where they are in three steps, finishes each with visible confirmation, checks exactly who gets what, then watches each email light up as it goes out. Failures show in rose and stay findable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Violet header with the CNCP logo, product name, sign out, theme toggle. Below it the title and one line of guidance on open sky, then a step rail with a sliding indicator and connectors that fill as steps complete. Solid cards hold the work. The primary action sits inside the step: upload first, Send in a sticky bar on the last step.

FORM: Pinned by the user (galaxy/space, purple). concept-seed ran after the first build for the record only; its assignment was not used, because a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Signature move: the send constellation, one star per recipient grouped by batch, igniting from the live send log. Motion grammar: slow compositor-only sky drift, 150-280ms interface transitions, ignite on completion, all disabled under reduced motion.
